# HabitPet Privacy Policy (DRAFT — for your review)

**Last updated: [date]**

HabitPet ("the app") is a habit-tracking app. This policy explains what data
we collect, why, and how it's handled.

## No sign-up required

HabitPet creates an account automatically the first time you open it — you
are never asked to enter personal information to use the app. You can
optionally "link" your account to an email or phone number later, so your
data can be recovered on a new device.

## What we collect

- **Account info**: a username and display name (auto-generated unless you
  change them), and an email or phone number only if you choose to link your
  account.
- **Habit data**: the habits you create and the progress you log for them.
- **Step/walking data**: with your permission, your daily step count (via
  Health Connect on Android or HealthKit on iOS, or your device's built-in
  step sensor as a fallback) to power the in-app walking challenge. This is
  used only for that feature — never for advertising, never shared with
  third parties, never sold.
- **Social data**: friend connections, friend requests, and nudges/cheers
  you send to friends, visible only to the people you've connected with.
- **Push notification token**: only if you enable notifications, so we can
  deliver them to your device.

## What we don't do

We don't run ads, use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, or sell your
data to anyone. Nothing you log is used for anything beyond making the app
work.

## Who can see your data

Your habit progress and streaks are visible only to the friends you
explicitly add inside the app. Nobody else — including us — looks at your
individual data except as needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored on secure, industry-standard cloud infrastructure and
transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time
from Profile → Delete account. This takes effect immediately and cannot be
undone.

## Children's privacy

HabitPet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after
a change means you accept the updated policy.
